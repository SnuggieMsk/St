B · Company history — the 75-year arc from TI Cycles to Murugappa engineering flagship

B.1 Founding (1949) — British TI + Murugappa JV

TI Cycles of India founded 1949 as a JV between Tube Investments plc (UK) and the Murugappa Chettiar group[955]. Manufactured bicycles under BSA / Hercules / Philips brands — brands that defined the Indian cycle market through the 1950s-60s. Tube Products of India was incorporated separately for industrial precision tubes.

B.2 Murugappa control (1959-1990s) — Indianisation + diversification

The two companies merged in 1959 to form integrated Tube Investments of India. Built India's first cold-rolled-steel-strip + precision-tubes capability and the largest bicycle distribution network through 1970s-80s. By the late 1980s, Murugappa Group (via Ambadi Investments) had bought out the British TI plc stake.

B.3 Demerger and re-listing (2006-2017)

The current entity (CIN L35100TN2008PLC069496)[955] was incorporated 06 Oct 2008 as part of a Murugappa restructuring — demerging the investment + financial-services arm (now Cholamandalam Holdings) from the engineering business. TII relisted on BSE+NSE in 2017.

B.4 The 2010s — auto-component scale + global tier-1 supply

Built scale in auto-component precision tubes (door-impact beams, propeller shafts, axle tubes) supplying Stellantis, Ford, Renault, GM, plus all Indian OEMs. Tier-1 supplier with Tier-2 export capability. Bicycles continued domestic + emerging-market export. Engineering division added cold-rolled stainless tube + boiler tube grades.

B.5 The EV pivot (2020-2024) — TI Clean Mobility

The structural inflection. TI Clean Mobility announced 2021 with initial capex Rs ~750 Cr[956] for e-3W passenger (Montra Eviator) + e-3W cargo (Montra Cargo) + e-tractor (via 2024 Cellestial E-Mobility acquisition). IPLTech Electric for e-CV; Jayem Automotives + TIVOLT formed broader EV platform. By FY25, TII emerged as the most credible domestic challenger to Mahindra Last Mile Mobility + Bajaj e-3W in the small-commercial-EV cluster.

B.6 Next 36 months (FY26-FY29) — capex window + family transition

FY24 revenue Rs 16,890 Cr; analyst-est FY28 Rs 30,000 Cr (CAGR ~12%, EV-business swing factor). EV-capex envelope Rs 400-700 Cr. Family transition: Vellayan Subbiah is Group Executive Chairman of Murugappa overall; M.A.M. Arunachalam (Aru) is TII Chairman; Mukesh Ahuja became MD in 2024 — signals more aggressive growth + capex-deployment phase.

B.7 Why this matters for the IBank pitch

(1) Murugappa is a builder-group, not a trader-group — TII has reinvested across every cycle. (2) The EV-mobility play is engineering execution, not greenfield speculation. (3) The 5-flagship Murugappa structure makes TII the entry-point to a Rs 75,000 Cr group-treasury opportunity (TII + CUMI + EID Parry + Coromandel + Chola Finance). The bank that wins TII gets cross-sell into all five.

D · Value-chain — precision-tubes + auto-components + EV-mobility cluster

TII operates across three distinct value-chains under one listed entity: (1) precision-steel-tubes (HRC steel → cold-rolled strip → cold-drawn precision tube → auto-component fabrication); (2) bicycles (steel + components → frame fab → assembly → BSA/Hercules/Philips brand → 5,000+ dealer outlets); (3) EV mobility (battery cell + e-motor + chassis → e-3W / e-tractor / e-CV → fleet customers + last-mile-mobility operators).

I · Objection handling

"We are happy with our current banks (SBI + HDFC lead)."

"Mr. Aru — we are not asking to displace anyone. The Rs 400-700 Cr TI Clean Mobility capex window is incremental and partially greenfield (subsidiary-level debt). We are asking for an anchor or co-anchor seat on that sleeve specifically. Not displacing, adding to the consortium for a new ticket size."

"Your rates are not competitive vs PSU."

"Capex TL rates are within 10-15 bps. Differentiation: sustainability-linked covenant gives 5 bps step-down on EV-revenue-mix; LME-steel IRS at scale that PSU cannot price; 7-day term-sheet vs PSU 21-28 days; family-PB platform PSU does not have. Structure, not headline rate."

"TI Clean Mobility is still loss-making — we need profitability proof first."

"Exactly the structuring opportunity. Sustainability-linked covenant ties our pricing to your profitability path: when EV-revenue-mix crosses 25% (correlated with EBITDA breakeven), margin steps down 5 bps. We share the risk in a structurally honest way."

"Murugappa family wealth is private."

"That is exactly the design point of our PB platform — we are most discrete on India's senior promoter PB. Our partner can fly down for a 30-minute conversation about how families like yours have institutionalised wealth without losing control. No commitment."

"You are too small to lead our group treasury."

"We do not need to lead group treasury today — we want anchor on TII subsidiary-EV capex. Once we deliver on Phase-1 commissioning, the group treasury conversation becomes natural at the FY28 refresh."

"Your turnaround time on credit decisions is slow."

"Weekly committee with 7-day SLA on standard tickets. We will write SLA into the term-sheet."
