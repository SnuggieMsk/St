F · Question bank — what to ask, what NOT to ask

12 questions for the meeting

  1. What is your PLI 2.0 commitment delta vs PLI 1.0?
  2. What % of BOM is China-imported and how is FX hedged?
  3. What is the SMT-line capex sequencing FY26-29?
  4. How is the brand-OEM receivable cycle structured (Apple/Xiaomi/Samsung)?
  5. What is the FX hedge ratio + tenor of forward book?

What NOT to ask

  • Do not ask for share of wallet on first meeting — signals begging.
  • Do not ask "what is your debt outstanding" — we should know this from registry.
  • Do not ask "who is your lead bank" — we should know this.
  • Do not ask for the audited financials — CFO will share if relationship deepens.
  • Do not pitch on rate — PSU/incumbent will always undercut.
G · Objection handling — the comebacks for the predictable pushback

Objection 1: "We are happy with our current banks."

We are not asking to displace anyone — we are asking for a slot on the next refresh / capex tranche where we bring something differentiated (capex speed, FX depth, sustainability covenant, PB platform). Eight banks already share your wallet; one more on the right product is normal.

Objection 2: "Your rates are not competitive vs PSU."

Rates are commoditised within 10-15 bps. The differentiation is execution speed, structural pricing on derivatives, sustainability-linked covenants that give you a margin step-down, and a retail/PB/TASC bundle PSUs cannot offer. We compete on structure, not headline rate.

Objection 3: "You are too small to lead our capex consortium."

We do not need to lead — we need a co-anchor seat. Once we deliver on Phase-1, the FY28 refresh becomes a different conversation. Show us one Rs 100-200 Cr sleeve where we can prove capex execution speed.

Objection 4: "You don't have a sugar/auto/EMS-specialist team."

We have a electronics manufacturing services (ems)-cluster team in Mumbai/Chennai with 8-10 senior bankers including the relationship lead, capex underwriter, FX dealer, and PB partner. Their CVs are in your pre-read.

Objection 5: "Your turnaround time on credit decisions is slow."

Our committee meets weekly with a 7-day SLA on standard ticket sizes. PSU peers run 21-28 day cycles. We will write that SLA into our term-sheet.

Objection 6: "You don't understand our family / promoter dynamics."

Our PB platform manages over Rs 50,000 Cr of family wealth across India's top 200 industrial families. Senior PB partner can fly down for a no-commitment first meeting any week.

"MD — thank you for the time. We are not here for share-of-wallet. We have studied the FY25 print and the FY26 capex commentary, and we have three structural observations — one wholesale, one treasury, one family. If at the end of 30 minutes you do not see value, we leave it there."

L.2 Mid-call (3 blocks, 15 min each)

Block 1 (Wholesale): "On the Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) cluster the FY26-29 driver is pli / specs extension. We have a structured term-sheet ready — can we walk through?"

Block 2 (Treasury): "RBI at 6.50% with June MPC consensus 25 bps cut. We have an IRS structure that locks 60% of WCDL at the post-cut rate — saves Rs 0-0 Cr/yr. Want to see numbers?"

Block 3 (Family + group): "Separately — would it be useful to have our senior PB partner come down from Mumbai once, just to walk you through how multi-generational mandates are structured at our platform? No commitment."

L.3 The ask (5 min)

"Three things we would like to take away today: (1) permission to put the term-sheet in front of CFO in 14 days; (2) a 1-hour treasury session in May; (3) a soft introduction for a 30-minute family/PB conversation. If we get one of these, this meeting was worth it."

L.4 Follow-up (T+24 hrs)

  • One-page meeting recap email — three asks, three commitments, three deliverables, dates.
  • Sustainability-linked TL one-pager attached.
  • IRS-structure sample term-sheet attached.
  • PB-partner profile attached.
  • CC: Group MD, CFO, Foundation/TASC trustee secretary (if applicable).

L.5 What NOT to do

  • Do not pitch on rate. Incumbent will always undercut on flagship product.
  • Do not show up without the term-sheet draft. The single biggest signal we send is "we are ready, incumbent is not".
  • Do not propose climb-to-lead in this meeting. That is FY29 conversation.
