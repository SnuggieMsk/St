Ford India Private Limited
US-parent GCC + export-services entity post-2021 India-manufacturing exit

Ford India Private Limited (CIN U34103TN2000PTC045537)[130] is the Ford Motor Company (USA) subsidiary that historically operated the 350-acre Maraimalai Nagar manufacturing complex near Chennai. Following the September 2021 announcement of exit from Indian automotive manufacturing[131], the entity pivoted to (a) Ford Business Solutions (FBS), the Chennai-headquartered global capability centre employing 10,000+ engineering and business-operations FTEs, (b) spare-parts support and after-sales service obligations, and (c) a residual export-manufacturing option under evaluation with the Tamil Nadu Industries Department[132]. Sibling entity Ford Motor Private Limited (CIN U74120TN1998PTC041070)[133] houses the substantive global-capability-centre operations and carries a higher credit rating. FY25 TOI at FIPL is Rs 6,121 Cr[128] with PAT of Rs 710 Cr — PAT > EBITDA reflects one-time gains from the Tata Motors Sanand asset transaction wind-down[134] and tax reversals. Zero open charges on the MCA register[126] — nine Indian-bank consortium lines are all unsecured / intra-group funded. This is a clean acquisition pitch: the incumbent consortium is on unsecured terms, and the narrative reframe is “grow wholesale share of a structurally de-risked GCC” rather than “displace a secured incumbent”.

The June MPC rate-lock window — why this matters to every Tier-1 relationship

The next MPC is 4–6 June 2026. Goldman's pricing is 50 bp hike; the street consensus is 25 bp. Either way, any Tier-1 working capital renewal, term loan sanction, or NCD issuance that closes before the MPC books the current 5.25% repo — the MCLR / T-bill linkage saves 25–50 bp over a typical 3–5 year tenor. Every dossier below carries a '30-day rate-lock' action in Section 14 for this exact reason.

  • Specialty chemicals, agrochem, polymers: input cost pass-through lag → 150–250 bp gross margin compression in FY27 Q1; short-term working-capital gap opens.

  • Thermal IPPs: coal shortage tail risk if hydro under-delivers; merchant tariffs firm Rs 0.50–1.20/kWh above LTA.

US–India trade deal: the 31 July / 29 September cliff

The July framework compressed US reciprocal tariffs from 50% → 18% on most lines; generic pharma exports at 0%, patented at 100%.[6] Electronics / smartphones / auto-comp land at 15–18%. Two working deadlines: 31 Jul 2026 (generic pharma zero-duty shipments clear US ports) and 29 Sep 2026 (patented molecule 100% tariff goes live, forcing a onshore-manufacture vs re-export decision for branded players). Working-capital cycle tightens by 30–45 days for exporters who need to front-load Q2 shipments.

Rupee regime

04.3 Notable items in the FY25 P&L shape

The reported PAT of Rs 710 Cr on EBITDA of Rs 626 Cr is anomalous and is explained by three components (each a diligence item for precise quantification at credit-committee):

  1. One-time gain on Tata-Motors Sanand transaction residuals[134] — Ford sold its Sanand plant to Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles in Aug 2022 for ~Rs 725 Cr[139]; final earn-out / warranty-release tranches concluding in FY24-FY25 may have added Rs 80–140 Cr to other income.
  2. MAT / deferred-tax reversals — post-exit tax-loss utilisation and MAT-credit adjustments.
  3. Asset-sale gains (Maraimalai Nagar equipment + scrap) — equipment decommissioning + export of certain tooling to Ford global network.

06.3 India auto-manufacturing restart optionality

The residual Maraimalai Nagar 350-acre complex carries a structural option value under two thesis paths:

  1. Export-only SOP (most likely outcome) — Ford re-starts passenger-vehicle assembly exclusively for export markets (Africa + SAARC + Middle East). Tamil Nadu Industries Department (TIDCO) has reportedly signalled willingness to negotiate a revised incentive package[132]. Likely timing: announcement FY27 if restart proceeds; SOP FY28. Capex envelope Rs 800–1,400 Cr.
  2. EV-pivot (lower probability) — Ford's global EV strategy is scaled back post-2024 re-plan; India EV-manufacturing restart is lower probability vs the Ford Pro commercial-vehicle route. Diligence item: monitor Ford global capital-allocation commentary at Q3 CY26 earnings.
  3. Sale / partial-sale (tail risk) — the Maraimalai Nagar land asset at current Kancheepuram industrial-plot prices (~Rs 6–8 Cr / acre) is worth Rs 2,100–2,800 Cr on a hypothetical disposal. A Tata Motors-style transaction is possible if the export thesis does not materialise by FY28.

The GCC-led component of the Ford India relationship is stable, high-credit-quality, and scalable with employee growth. Three discount guardrails

Bundling discount

At most 8 bp discount off any individual floor when client commits ≥ 3 products of significant size. Below 3 products, no discount.

Relationship-duration discount

5 bp for multi-year commitment (≥ 3 years); only on rolling products (WC / EPC); not on new TLs.

Upfront-arrangement discount

3 bp reduction on arranger fees for upfront-subscribed tranches (reduces distribution risk). Not permitted on BRLM or IPO.

These floors have been tested against actual executed deals for similarly-rated (AA- to A1+) South-India industrial counterparties in FY25–FY26. Pricing below these levels is observed in only 3% of historical deals, typically where the bank is purchasing league-table credit or political capital.

For entities with clear Phase 2 capex pipelines, issue a commitment-letter pre-emption: IBank commits to arrange the Phase 2 facility (subject to customary conditions) at Phase 1 sign. The option-value of the Phase 2 commitment often unlocks Phase 1 at market-competitive pricing.

Escalation 4 — Co-arranger pivot (preserve optionality)

If sole-arranger role is lost to the incumbent, pivot to 40-50% co-arranger participation. This preserves the relationship-currency for the next cycle (typically 18-24 months out) while ensuring IBank captures proportional wholesale wallet. Never exit the consortium entirely over a single facility.

Escalation 5 — PB / family-office channel

For founder-led / family-owned entities, even if wholesale lags, direct PB engagement with the promoter family often re-opens the corporate relationship. Wealth-management mandates for promoter-family individuals typically precede corporate banking decisions by 12-24 months.
