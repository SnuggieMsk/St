B · Sector value-chain — where Bannari sits and what flows through

Sugar-cane is a three-output raw material: sucrose → sugar; molasses (B+C) → ethanol; bagasse + press-mud → co-gen power + bio-fertiliser. Cane-to-mill price is regulated (TN-SAP[15] + central FRP); sugar-MSP is regulated; ethanol off-take price is GoI-fixed under EBP-2025 (E20 mandate[12]); co-gen power price is regulated by TNERC. Bannari operates 5 sugar mills + 200 KLPD distillery + 92 MW co-gen — fully integrated.

B.2 What this means for the wallet

The working-capital cycle at Bannari is bimodal. Sugar drags ~120-150 days of cycle (cane payment to sugar realisation). Ethanol shrinks cycle to ~30-45 days. The mix shift to ethanol (target 60% of revenue by FY28 vs ~38% FY25) is structurally WC-cycle accretive — meaning peak CC utilisation will fall as ethanol mix rises. This argues for capex TL today (to fund the shift), then CC right-sizing in FY28. The IBank pitch on capex is: "We fund your shift, and at refresh we right-size the CC together — fairer pricing for both."

E · Five conversation hooks — the narratives the RM walks in with

One hook = one walking-in story. Pick which to lead with based on who is in the room. Each is sized as: opening line, the pivot question, the data-point, the structure to drop.

Hook 1 — The defence (lead if speaking to current treasury head / CFO)

Opening: "Mr. Murugan, FY27 refresh comes up Sep-Oct — HDFC + PNB will both push for refresh-and-grow. We are at #3 with Rs 100 Cr; we want to refresh same-tenor and bid an additional Rs 50-60 Cr to climb to #2 alongside PNB. The reason is one we both already know: ethanol is changing your WC mix, and we want to be on the right side of the capex when it lands."

Pivot: "What is HDFC indicating on next refresh? Are they pricing bank-loan or moving you to NCD?"

Data-point: CARE AA-Stable Dec 2025[582]; Rs 500 Cr LT facilities + Rs 15 Cr LT/ST; ratings cushion still holds room for AA+ if ethanol mix improves.

Structure to drop: Rs 50-60 Cr WCDL refresh tranche; pari-passu; 12-month review; 0% prepayment; sustainability-linked margin step-down (10 bps) at first KPI breach.

Hook 2 — The ethanol capex (lead if S.V. or strategy team in the room)

Opening: "Sir, you announced 200→350-400 KLPD in the FY26 annual report and the Reddy interview. Government EBP-25 is now an irreversible policy — oil-import bill is the politics, ethanol is the answer. The capex is Rs 200-400 Cr depending on whether you go 350 or 400; we want the anchor seat with a 5-bank syndicate including HDFC + PNB. We bring an 8-year tenor + 18-month moratorium + sustainability-linked covenant + 10 bps step-down for E20-blending KPI."

Pivot: "Have you locked the EPC partner yet? When is Board approval scheduled?"

Data-point: EBP-2025 ethanol off-take pricing [12] held Rs 65-72/litre; OMC payment cycle 30-45 days; ethanol gross margin 18-22% vs sugar 6-9% (mix-shift adds 400-600 bp at EBITDA-margin level by FY28).

Structure to drop: Rs 250 Cr / 8-year / 18-month moratorium / SOFR+330 floating with annual reset / sustainability-linked KPI: E20 blending crossing 18% by FY27 and 22% by FY28 unlocks 10 bps step-down each.

Hook 3 — The PB / family-wealth (lead if wanting senior-leadership rapport, partner with PB-RM)

Pivot: "Who today helps with multi-generation planning?"

Data-point: Founder + family-trust + senior leadership = Rs 1,500-1,800 Cr addressable wealth at current Bannari Sugars + BASM market cap. Realistic PB capture on liquid portfolio 20-25% → Rs 250-380 Cr AUM.

Structure to drop: Senior-PB partner introduction; bespoke advisory; family-trust + endowment custody; structured-product access (NCDs, AIFs, GIFT-IFSC).

Hook 4 — The 30,000 cane farmer ecosystem (lead if speaking to Bannari Foundation / CSR / rural team)

We can convert that into a Kisan-credit + cane-supply-anchored salary-CASA pipeline that satisfies your CSR + farmer-welfare goals AND adds Rs 200-300 Cr of farmer-deposit float to the system. F · Question bank — 12 questions to pull intelligence + signal expertise

Each question doubles as (a) intelligence-gathering, (b) demonstrating you understand the business deeper than a transactional banker would.

  1. "What is your view on the cane-SAP outlook for 2026-27 season — is the Rs 3,650/T formula sustainable if monsoon misses?" — signals you understand TN-SAP[15] dynamics + drought sensitivity. Pulls their FY27 P&L bias.
  2. "How are you sizing 350 vs 400 KLPD on the distillery expansion? Is the upper end limited by cane availability or by EPC schedule?" — signals you understand capex-sizing trade-offs. Pulls capex magnitude + trigger.
  3. "What is your blended ethanol off-take mix — B-heavy molasses, C-heavy molasses, sugarcane-juice direct? Which lane gives you the best EBITDA per litre?" — signals deep ethanol-feedstock knowledge. Pulls product-mix optimisation logic.
  4. "Has the OMC payment cycle held at 30-45 days post-LPS[22] reform, or have you seen any backsliding from BPCL/IOC/HPCL?" — signals you understand the only real risk in ethanol-receivable. Pulls receivable-cycle anxiety.
  5. "On the Karnataka mill — is the bottleneck cane availability, crushing-capacity, or distillery-feedstock allocation?" — signals you read their geographic operations. Pulls operating-leverage thesis.
  6. "What is your Debt-Service-Coverage covenant headroom for the next two refresh cycles — 1.5x or tighter?" — signals you read their credit-rating rationale. Pulls covenant-pressure tolerance.
  7. "How is the founder-family thinking about succession — are second-gen now in operational roles, or is treasury still SV-driven?" — signals you respect promoter-family dynamics. Pulls the PB opportunity.
  8. "BASM + Sakthi Sugars — are these treated as separate banking entities, or is there appetite for a group-level relationship pricing structure?" — signals you understand group-architecture. Pulls cross-sell willingness.
  9. "On co-gen — have you considered open-access power sale to TN industrial customers, or are you continuing TANGEDCO PPA?" — signals you understand power-segment options. Pulls strategic flexibility.
  10. "FY27-28 capex pipeline beyond ethanol — granite expansion? Bio-fertiliser? Cement? Where would you want incremental balance-sheet flexibility?" — signals you understand group-capex roadmap. Pulls the next ask.
  11. "How are you thinking about the cotton-textile sister BASM under EU CBAM/EUDR[18] — is that a Bannari-Sugars problem or a BASM-board problem?" — signals you can see the entire group. Pulls cross-sell consent.
  12. "If we structured this with a 5 bps step-down on annual E20-blending KPI, would your treasury team value that — or would they prefer pricing certainty?" — signals you can construct sustainability-linked structures. Pulls structuring-preference.

F.2 What NOT to ask

  • Anything that sounds like you have not read their AR (eg. "What is your installed crushing capacity?" — it is published).
  • "Have you considered NCD?" without a specific catalyst — sounds generic; CFO will say "we have HDFC for that".
  • "Why are you still with HDFC?" — relationship-disrespectful. Frame instead as "How is HDFC pricing on next refresh?"
  • Anything on cane-yield or rainfall they cannot control — they will deflect. Stay with downstream-controllable items.
G · Objection handling — the comebacks that hold the room

Objection 1: "We are happy with our current consortium — HDFC and PNB do everything we need."

Comeback: "Of course — HDFC has been your lead since the 2022 refresh. The reason we are here is not to replace anyone. We are at #3 today and we want to be at #2 at next refresh. The reason matters: we are bringing the ethanol-capex anchor seat and a sustainability-linked covenant that HDFC is unlikely to underwrite at the same pricing. The choice is not us versus HDFC — the choice is whether you let one bank dictate your capex-cost or you have a co-anchor."

Objection 2: "Ethanol-capex is locked already — final shortlist with HDFC + SBI."

Comeback: "Understood. Two things: (a) syndication is not yet financial-close, and the EPC + IDC absorption usually leaves room for a co-anchor at a 5-10% sleeve; (b) sustainability-linked structures are pricing tighter than vanilla TLs in current market — we can probably bring 8-12 bps tighter on the same rating with the E20-blending KPI. Will you let us send a 1-page indicative term-sheet by Friday so you can compare?"

Objection 6: "Sustainability-linked covenants are administrative overhead — we'd rather have plain vanilla."

The pricing benefit is real: 10 bps on Rs 250 Cr is Rs 25 lakh/year — over 8-year tenor that is Rs 2 Cr in covenant-pricing benefit, no operational change."
