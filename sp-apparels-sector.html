B · Company history — the 36-year arc from 1989 Tirupur partnership to listed kids+infant apparel exporter with UK+Sri Lanka subsidiaries

B.1 Founding (1989) — P. Sundararajan's Tirupur partnership

Promoted as a partnership firm by P. Sundararajan in 1989 in the Tirupur knitwear cluster of Tamil Nadu — India's "Banian City" producing 50%+ of the country's knitwear exports. Second-wave Tirupur entrepreneur building integrated knitwear export business for Western brand customers.

B.2 The 1990s — Integrated knitwear export capability

Grew from small partnership to integrated knitwear-export entity. Strategic moat: end-to-end vertical integration — raw cotton/yarn → knitting → dyeing → cutting → stitching → finishing → packaging → export logistics. Compared favourably to standard Tirupur fragmented sub-contracting. Plants expanded across Avinashi and Tirupur township.

B.3 The 2000s — Kids+infants niche + first global brand customers

Carved out a specific niche in infant and children's knitted apparel (bodysuits, sleepsuits, tops, bottoms, dresses). Kids+infant apparel has higher safety + quality + chemical-compliance requirements creating a higher-margin segment with stickier customer relationships. Major customers added: Disney, Carter's, Babies R Us, Mothercare, Pumpkin Patch, OshKosh, Walmart, Target.

B.4 Incorporation (2005) + Crocodile India retail (2010s)

Partnership incorporated as S.P. Apparels Limited on 10 November 2005 as public limited company. Retail-apparel division eventually hived off into separate subsidiary S.P. Retail Ventures Limited (SPRV) marketing apparel under the Crocodile India brand.

B.5 IPO 2016 — BSE+NSE listing

Listed on BSE 540048 / NSE SPAL in 2016 raising growth capital + providing partial-exit liquidity to P. Sundararajan family. Promoter holding remained at 61.81% — among the more closely-held listed Tirupur knitwear exporters.

B.6 The 2020-2024 transformation — Sri Lanka + UK + revenue surge

Structural inflection. FY24 revenue Rs 1,087 Cr → FY25 Rs 1,395 Cr (+28% YoY). FY24 SPAIPL (Sri Lanka) incorporated — accessing GSP+ tariff preference for EU exports + diversifying away from TN production concentration. Apr 2026 saw SPUK (UK subsidiary, Rs 6.31 Cr) + further SPAIPL investment + Rs 27.96 Cr corporate guarantee for Jumeirah Lanka Pvt Ltd's HSBC loan.

B.7 Why this matters for the IBank pitch

(1) P. Sundararajan is a founder-builder — integrated vertical capability + global-brand customer base + 36-year continuous management. (2) Kids+infants niche is one of the stickiest apparel-export segments — safety/compliance moat creates pricing power. (3) FY25 +28% surge + Sri Lanka + UK signal active growth-deployment phase. (4) USD/GBP export-receivable book on Disney/Carter's/Walmart paper at investment-grade is structural factoring opportunity. (5) 61.81% closely-held promoter family + dual-generation Director cohort = sizeable family-PB. (6) Crocodile India retail + adult-apparel diversification create cross-sell.

D · Value-chain — Tirupur knitwear cluster + integrated vertical apparel-export pipeline

S.P. Apparels operates an end-to-end integrated knitwear export pipeline anchored on the Tirupur cluster. The cluster: raw cotton (open-end + ring-spun yarn from Tamil Nadu + Maharashtra spinners) → knitting (circular + flat knitting machines, Korean/German/Japanese equipment) → dyeing + finishing (in-house ZLD-compliant dyeing units) → cutting (CAD-driven; high efficiency) → stitching (~22,000 FTE across multiple lines) → finishing + packaging + label (per global brand specification) → export logistics (Chennai/Cochin/Tuticorin ports; Apparel Export Promotion Council documentation). India PLI textile (5,000 Cr scheme) + China+1 tailwind drive Tirupur cluster benefit.

H · Question bank

12 questions for the meeting

  1. FY25 +28% revenue surge — sustainable or one-time customer-orderbook recovery?
  2. Avinashi capacity utilisation FY26-29 + capex envelope split (knitting + dyeing + automation)?
  3. Sri Lanka GSP+ EU export ramp — what % of revenue target by FY28?
  4. UK SPUK strategy — what is the operating mandate (sales office, design + JV, manufacturing)?
  5. Jumeirah Lanka — what is the venture (hotel? other?); why the Rs 27.96 Cr HSBC corporate guarantee?
  6. Customer concentration — what % from top-3 customers (Disney, Carter's, Walmart)?
  7. Cotton + yarn procurement — Maharashtra spinners or TN; commodity hedge ratio?
  8. PLI Textile applicability — MMF + technical textile commitment?
  9. Crocodile India (SPRV) — outlet network today and FY28 target?
  10. Adult-apparel + private-label growth — outside core kids+infants?
  11. Sustainability covenant appetite — OEKO-TEX/GOTS/BCI etc?
  12. Family-PB — HUF/family-trust structure or individually held wealth?

What NOT to ask

  • Do not ask about specific brand-customer pricing (NDA-protected).
  • Do not pitch on rate — Tirupur exporters are tight on cost-discipline.
  • Do not propose family-succession + ESOP advisory in first meeting.
  • Do not name specific competitor banks unless asked.
  • Do not over-pitch capex displacing existing consortium — we want incremental cross-border + factoring sleeves.

"That's the precise reason for single-bank multi-currency consolidation. Our LKR + USD + INR cross-border + correspondent network manages payment + collection cycles with sanctions-clean routing. PSU peers cannot do Sri Lankan corporate-banking at this level."
